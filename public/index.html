Her Story

A journey of courage, reinvention, family and purpose.

Anjali Balwal is an Auckland-based entrepreneur, international pageant personality and community-focused woman whose journey reflects resilience, reinvention — and the belief that it is never too late to pursue a dream.

Born in Jammu & Kashmir and raised in Chandigarh, she grew up with strong family values and a deep connection to her cultural roots — valuing education, perseverance and staying grounded while pursuing ambitious goals. Drawn to New Zealand for its natural beauty, safety and quality of life, she completed an MBA at the University of Canterbury in Christchurch and began her career as a Business Manager.

Today she brings together entrepreneurship, fashion, culture, women’s empowerment and international representation — a modern woman who embraces her heritage while looking confidently toward the future.

The Journey

From Jammu & Kashmir to the world stage

  1. I

    Roots

    Born in Jammu & Kashmir and raised in Chandigarh in a traditional Rajput family — where education, family and perseverance came first.

  2. II

    Aotearoa

    A move to New Zealand, an MBA at the University of Canterbury in Christchurch, and a professional career as a Business Manager.

  3. III

    The Entrepreneur

    She founded Wine Chambers, a fine-dining restaurant on Auckland’s Shortland Street, welcoming notable guests including former Prime Ministers Helen Clark and Jacinda Ardern.

  4. IV

    The Turning Point

    The pandemic closed the restaurant — one of the hardest chapters of her life. Rather than let it define her, Anjali began rebuilding her confidence and searching for a new direction.

  5. V

    The Crown

    Selected from thousands of applicants, she represented New Zealand at Haut Monde Mrs India Worldwide 2025 in Dubai — crowned 1st Runner-Up, Australia/New Zealand Zone, and Mrs Iconic Eyes.

  6. VI

    Cannes

    In 2026 she was invited to walk the red carpet at the Festival de Cannes — carrying the story of a New Zealand-based Indian woman onto an international stage.

  7. VII

    The Judge’s Chair

    From contestant to recognised pageant personality: judging Mrs Cosmic 2026 in Mumbai and Haut Monde Mrs 2026 in Sydney, and mentoring the next generation of contestants.

  8. VIII

    Next Chapter

    Her own cosmetics brand is in development — beauty with purpose, made for women ready to begin again.

Family & Values

Ambition and family, growing together.

Despite her growing public profile, family remains at the centre of Anjali’s life. A devoted wife and mother, she credits her husband’s encouragement — when she was unsure about entering pageantry, and again when international opportunities arose — as the foundation of her journey.

Her story is proof that ambition does not have to come at the expense of family. With the right support, the two can flourish side by side.
